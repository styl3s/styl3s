# 30-Day Skin Retouching for Portraits — Training Outline

**Goal:** Be able to manually retouch a full portrait/wedding session to a
professional, industry-standard 20-photo gallery — without relying on Evoto —
in Photoshop (detail retouching) + Lightroom (base edits, culling, batch,
delivery).

**Pace:** 1 hour/day, 30 consecutive days.

**Tools:** Photoshop, Lightroom. Own RAW files available for practice.

## Diagnostic summary (baseline, established Day 0)

Two-round diagnostic (20 questions + 8 follow-up questions) before Day 01.

**Strengths (don't need dedicated time, just reinforcement in context):**
- Lightroom → Photoshop workflow ordering (global/local adjustments before detail work)
- Non-destructive workflow concepts (Smart Objects, never editing Background directly)
- Healing tool selection and edge-case judgment (Spot Healing/Healing Brush/Patch/Clone Stamp)
- Frequency separation theory (correctly identifies low=tone, high=texture, and what blur radius controls)
- Dodge & burn purpose (light/shadow sculpting, distinct from blemish removal)
- Tone consistency and "plastic skin" causes/avoidance, conceptually
- Teeth/eye whitening and flyaway-hair workflow placement, conceptually
- Precise, hands-on understanding of what Evoto automates vs. where it fails (clothes/background/hair highlights vs. FS/D&B)
- **Culling/selects**: already has a structured multi-pass process to get from a full shoot to a curated gallery — light touch only, fold into full pipeline, don't re-teach.

**Gaps to weight the plan toward:**
1. **Frequency separation execution** — only built it a few times following a tutorial; not fluent unassisted. Needs repetition to automatic.
2. **Dodge & burn technique breadth** — knows an inverted-high-pass-style method but not the standard 50% gray/Overlay method; needs both, plus judgment on when to use which.
3. **Layer masking** — basic brush masking only. Needs feathering, gradients, refine edge, and intro to luminosity masks (useful for both D&B and selective grading).
4. **Manual speed/confidence** — currently 30-60+ min per photo fully manual, self-rated 2-3/5. This is the core bottleneck to replacing Evoto professionally; needs deliberate speed drills, not just more theory.
5. **Color management** — can click the right export defaults by habit but can't yet explain sRGB vs. Adobe RGB/ProPhoto RGB or when to soft-proof for print. Needs a compact but real lesson, not just "keep doing what you're doing."
6. **Automation ownership** — currently uses other people's Photoshop actions/LR presets. Needs to build personal ones tuned to their own workflow, for real turnaround speed on a 20-photo gallery.

## Week 1 (Days 1-7): Foundations & Non-Destructive Fluency

| Day | Focus | Objectives |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Workflow architecture & personal template | Lock in LR→PS handoff order, build a reusable PSD template (Smart Object base, layer group structure), establish non-destructive discipline |
| 02 | Blemish removal tool mastery | Drill Spot Healing/Healing Brush/Patch/Clone Stamp; edge cases (hairline, jaw); speed pass |
| 03 | Layer masking deep dive | Feathering, gradients, refine edge; intro to luminosity masks |
| 04 | Frequency separation from scratch | Build FS unassisted (Gaussian blur, Apply Image, blend modes), no tutorial crutch; repeat 3x |
| 05 | Frequency separation practice | Apply across varied skin tones/lighting; diagnose radius/color-halo mistakes |
| 06 | Dodge & burn — Method A | 50% gray layer + Overlay/Soft Light, standard technique, practice on 3 photos |
| 07 | Dodge & burn — Method B + review | Refine the inverted-high-pass method already known; compare vs. Method A, decide when to use which; Week 1 recap |

## Week 2 (Days 8-14): Combining Skills + Speed Building

| Day | Focus | Objectives |
|---|---|---|
| 08 | Full combined workflow, baseline | FS + D&B + masking + healing on one photo, timed — establish real baseline |
| 09 | Speed drill | Repeat full workflow, target cutting time 20%; identify bottlenecks |
| 10 | Avoiding "plastic skin" | Texture preservation, grain sampling, opacity control, re-sharpening after smoothing |
| 11 | Eyes & teeth detail retouching | Natural whitening/brightening, catchlights, iris detail |
| 12 | Hair & background cleanup | Flyaways, background distractions, edge cleanup |
| 13 | Skin tone / color grading consistency | Matching WB and grade across photos via LR sync + PS color match |
| 14 | Timed challenge + Week 2 recap | 3 varied photos back-to-back, timed; compare to Day 08 baseline |

## Week 3 (Days 15-21): Output, Color Management & Batch Efficiency

| Day | Focus | Objectives |
|---|---|---|
| 15 | Color management theory | sRGB vs. Adobe RGB vs. ProPhoto RGB, gamut, soft proofing — fill the conceptual gap |
| 16 | Delivery export setup | Build LR export presets for web gallery vs. print lab; verify/monitor calibration |
| 17 | Pipeline integration | Fold existing culling process into full end-to-end pipeline timing (light touch — already a strength) |
| 18 | Build your own Photoshop Action | Record a personal action for FS scaffold + D&B layer setup, replacing reliance on others' actions |
| 19 | Build your own Lightroom presets | Batch/export presets and sync settings tuned to personal color-grading style |
| 20 | Mixed-conditions challenge | Apply full workflow + new personal actions across photos shot in different light |
| 21 | Refine & Week 3 recap | Fix friction points found in Days 18-20; finalize action/preset set |

## Week 4 (Days 22-30): Full Gallery Simulation & Professional Pace

| Day | Focus | Objectives |
|---|---|---|
| 22 | Session setup | Cull a real/mock session to a 20-photo gallery scope; set time budget per shot tier (hero vs. standard) |

| 25 | Photoshop batch 2 (photos 8-14) | Continue; focus on speed + consistency across different faces/skin tones |

| 27 | Color management & export pass | Soft-proof, convert, export full gallery for web (and print if applicable); calibration check |

| 29 | Timing & workflow retro | Compare total turnaround vs. professional benchmarks; refine personal actions/presets |
| 30 | Final SOP & portfolio piece | Assemble a before/after piece from the mock gallery; write a personal repeatable SOP for future weddings; program wrap-up |

## Session generation notes

Each day's session file is generated at the start of that day from this
outline plus what's been learned about Ben's progress in prior sessions
(the "What I Learned" summaries at the end of each day file). If a day's
diagnostic drill reveals a skill is solid earlier than planned, the next
day's session should compress/skip ahead rather than padding; if a gap
proves deeper than expected, insert extra reps before moving on.
